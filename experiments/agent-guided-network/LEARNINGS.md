# Learning index

Updated: 6 September 2026.
Scope: Evidence from this experiment, not established properties of general AI.
Intent owner: [EXPERIMENT.md](EXPERIMENT.md).

## Current goals

Primary mechanism, H01: an agent deliberately shapes numerical weights during learning and leaves a useful network that runs without that agent. The small studies demonstrate this participation mechanism. Their non-agent controls matching the result do not negate participation; they limit claims of a comparative advantage.

Next capability, H07: reuse one learned representation across unfamiliar objects, relations, event histories, and task combinations. Ultimate aim, H08: grounded natural-language conversation generated by the exported network itself. Neither is demonstrated by the three existing studies. [The world-language design](generalisation/WORLD_LANGUAGE.md) proposes how to investigate them.

H06 compression remains a separate objective: retained capability at smaller measured storage budgets. Hand placement alone does not imply compression. H02 superiority over conventional training remains a separate, unanswered comparison rather than the programme's primary success condition.

## Direction decisions, not experimental findings

D001, 6 September 2026: the user clarified participation as the intended mechanism and natural-language conversation as the ultimate aim. Prioritise generalisation through shared object, event, temporal, and quantity representations with language alongside them. This records intent, not newly observed evidence. Preserve L001-L018 and all raw results unchanged.

D002, 6 September 2026: distinguish ordinary numerical world/dialogue memory from the removed training agent's conversation history. A deployed network may remember its own inputs in declared state. Generated dialogue, hard-coded sentence rendering, and externally generated answers are different outcomes and must be labelled separately.

## How to extend this index

Give each finding a stable ID, a falsifiable statement, an observed/proposed/inconclusive status, a scope limit, and a link to immutable run evidence. Record contradictory results and rejected updates. Update the index after a completed observation or study, not on the strength of an agent explanation.

Keep parameter counts, active weights, dtype, metadata, code bytes, and complete deployed bytes separate. Keep one physical assistant, logical node roles, fresh decisions, and deterministic replay separate. Unknown conversation cost remains unknown.

## Questions still open

Can shared concepts support unfamiliar combinations and longer histories without new weight changes? Does joint world-and-language teaching support generated multi-turn conversation rather than selecting prepared replies? Can the model distinguish what happened from what was observed, handle corrections, and ask for missing information? None has been measured here.

Compactness, less-structured transfer, and superiority to automatic structure selection remain secondary research questions. The compression study confirms a compact task-specific solution but its non-agent control matches it. That is compatible with the demonstrated participation mechanism and does not establish broad intelligence. Keep new direction decisions separate from empirical ledger entries until runs supply evidence.
